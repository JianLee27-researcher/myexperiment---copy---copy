Study Debrief
{% if has_ai %}

This study examined how AI transparency and which team role is AI-assisted affect trust and decision-making in two-person procurement teams. Participants were randomly assigned to either a high-transparency (Glass Box) or low-transparency (Black Box) AI condition, or to a human-only control condition without AI.

The AI recommendation system was a controlled simulation (Wizard of Oz design). Across the study as a whole, the AI is correct about 70% of the time on average. {{ debrief_accuracy_text }}

This study examined how AI transparency and which team role is AI-assisted affect trust and decision-making in two-person procurement teams. Some participants were randomly assigned to work with an AI recommendation system; your team was assigned to the control condition, making decisions without any AI involvement.

Your team's data serves as an important baseline: it lets us see how procurement teams make decisions and calibrate trust in each other when no AI system is involved at all, so that we can meaningfully interpret what changes (if anything) when an AI recommendation system is introduced in other sessions.
